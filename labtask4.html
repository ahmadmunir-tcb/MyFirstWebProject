Record Breaker

Salah holds the record for the most goals scored in a 38-game Premier League season (32 goals). His consistency over multiple seasons at Liverpool has cemented his legacy as an unmatched winger in English football history.

Playmaker & Goalscorer

Unlike traditional wingers, Salah combines lethal finishing with elite vision. He is part of the exclusive club of players with 193 goals and 94 assists in the Premier League.
